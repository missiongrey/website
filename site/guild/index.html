The Mission Grey Guild

Human expertise, built into the intelligence system.

The Mission Grey Guild is a curated network of domain and regional experts whose knowledge feeds analysis, structured assessments, and high‑impact decisions. It is part of the intelligence process when the question requires it, not an advisory board beside it.
