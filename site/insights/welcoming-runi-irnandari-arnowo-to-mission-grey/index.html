We are honored to welcome Runi Irnandari Arnowo to Mission Grey.

Runi brings more than three decades of leadership experience from some of the world's leading financial institutions, including Deutsche Bank and Citi, with a career spanning corporate banking, transaction banking, capital markets, treasury solutions, financial institutions and fintech across Asia-Pacific.

Most recently, she served as Managing Director at Deutsche Bank, where she held responsibilities including Head of USMNC Regional Coverage Bankers APAC and Head of Relationship and Transaction Management ASEAN & Australia. In these roles, she led strategic client relationships and transaction banking initiatives across one of the world's most dynamic economic regions, positioned at the intersection of global trade, capital flows and economic transformation.

Throughout her career, Runi has worked closely with multinational corporations, financial institutions and regional businesses navigating increasingly complex operating environments shaped by shifting supply chains, evolving trade relationships and changing geopolitical realities.

As payments, trade, capital flows and financial infrastructure become ever more interconnected with geopolitics and regulation, organizations require a deeper understanding of the external forces shaping markets and business decisions. Runi's experience and perspective will further strengthen Mission Grey's capabilities in financial services, international trade and external intelligence across Asia-Pacific and beyond.

We are delighted to welcome Runi to Mission Grey and look forward to working together as we continue building the external intelligence infrastructure for decision-makers operating in an increasingly complex world.
