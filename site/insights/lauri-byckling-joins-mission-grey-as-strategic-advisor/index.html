Lauri Byckling Joins Mission Grey as Strategic Advisor

By Mission GreyPublished June 9, 2026Category Strategy

Mission Grey is pleased to announce that Lauri Byckling has joined the company as Strategic Advisor. He spent decades at Deloitte, where he held several senior leadership positions.

Lauri has more than 30 years of experience helping large organizations develop strategy, improve operations, and use technology in practical business decision-making. He started his consulting career at Accenture and later spent decades at Deloitte, where he held several senior leadership positions, including Managing Partner roles in Finland and across the Nordic region. During his career, he has worked with international companies, leadership teams, and major transformation programs across multiple industries and markets.

Lauri Byckling brings deep experience turning complex services into practical solutions that companies can actually use. He has extensive experience in understanding executive and board-level priorities, building scalable advisory and technology services, and helping organizations adopt new operational capabilities.

Commenting on joining Mission Grey, Lauri Byckling said: “For business leaders, understanding what is happening outside the company is just as important as understanding what happens inside it. I am excited about the potential of Mission Grey to help organizations understand change, navigate uncertainty and turn information into insight and insight into action. With my experience in strategy, business transformation and technology, I look forward to supporting Mission Grey’s international growth journey and helping organizations navigate an increasingly complex world.”

Mission Grey is building a new type of External Intelligence platform that helps organizations understand what is happening outside the company and what it means for their business. The company combines global data sources, AI, business-specific intelligence models, and human expertise to monitor geopolitical developments, regulation, markets, supply chains, security risks, and other external factors that increasingly affect business operations and strategic decision-making.

The goal is not only to collect information, but to help leaders understand what matters, what may happen next, and how different scenarios could impact their organization and industry globally.

Mission Grey CEO Lauri Tähtinen commented: “Lauri has exceptional experience in working with major organizations, leadership teams, and technology-driven transformation. He understands both the strategic and practical side of building services that create real value for customers internationally.

Mission Grey is developing something genuinely new: a way to combine external data, AI, human expertise, and business understanding into one intelligence capability that helps organizations make better decisions in a rapidly changing world. As external uncertainty becomes a critical business issue globally, Lauri’s experience and perspective are extremely valuable for Mission Grey’s next phase of international growth.
