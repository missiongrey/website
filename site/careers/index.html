Careers at Mission Grey

Work with Mission Grey

High standards. Global perspectives. Real opportunities to make an impact.

Mission Grey brings together entrepreneurs, intelligence professionals, researchers, business leaders and technology experts from around the world.

We are building a new generation of external intelligence solutions that help organizations understand global change and make better decisions.

We have an ambitious vision, and we look for people who want to contribute to making it a reality.

Entrepreneurial by nature

Your contribution matters.

Mission Grey is not a traditional organization where people simply fill predefined roles.

We value initiative, independent thinking, responsibility and the ability to turn ideas into tangible results. Everyone is expected to bring something valuable to the business, whether through expertise, technology, new ideas, customer relationships or execution.

We offer opportunities to take ownership, develop new capabilities and help shape how the business grows.

We appreciate ambitious ideas, but what matters is the ability to make them happen.

Different backgrounds. Shared ambition.

Exceptional people don't all follow the same path.

One of Mission Grey's strengths is the diversity of experience, expertise and perspectives across our team and the Mission Grey Guild.

Our people have worked in international business, intelligence agencies, government, academia, consulting, finance, technology and entrepreneurship. They are based across different countries and regions, bringing perspectives shaped by very different professional and personal experiences.

Some have built companies. Others have developed advanced technologies, advised major organizations, conducted research or worked on some of the world's most complex challenges.

What connects them is not a common career path, but intellectual curiosity, high professional standards and a shared ambition to build something exceptional.

Global by design

Work across borders. Deliver results.

Mission Grey operates internationally, with people and activities across the Americas, Europe and Asia.

We believe talented people should not be limited by geography. Many of our opportunities can be pursued remotely, and we welcome people from different countries and backgrounds.

We value flexibility, independence and international collaboration. But flexibility comes with responsibility. We measure contributions through the quality of work, the value created and the results delivered, rather than physical presence in an office.

Remote arrangements and eligible locations may vary depending on the position and applicable requirements.

Show us what you can do.

We know that exceptional people do not always fit conventional job descriptions.

If you believe you can contribute something valuable to Mission Grey, we would like to hear from you, even if we are not currently advertising a position that exactly matches your background.

Tell us who you are, what you have done and, most importantly, what you believe you could help us achieve.

We welcome people at different career stages and from different professional backgrounds. We are committed to equal opportunities and evaluating candidates on their capabilities, potential and ability to contribute.
