We respect your privacy. We believe you, as a user of the Mission Grey website (the "Mission Grey Site"), should always know what data we collect from you and how we use it. You should also know that you have meaningful control over both. With respect to information submitted or collected on the Mission Grey Site, by using the Mission Grey Site, you accept the Privacy Policy set forth below. This Privacy Policy does not pertain to, and Mission Grey is not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party websites to which the Mission Grey Site hyperlinks.

1. General Data Collection Principles

Mission Grey Privacy Policy is based on the principles enshrined in California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Mission Grey collects two kinds of information on the Mission Grey Site: contact details you give us (names, surnames, and email addresses), which Mission Grey uses only for sending its newsletters and other information related to Mission Grey's activities; and information about how the site is used, described in section 3. Mission Grey does not share or sell either to third parties.

2. Personally Identifiable Information

The Mission Grey Site is designed so that you may browse it without providing any Personally Identifiable Information (defined below). However, certain areas of the Mission Grey Site may require or allow the voluntary submission of Personally Identifiable Information (for example, registration to receive the Mission Grey newsletter or other publications).

"Personally Identifiable Information" means information that would allow someone to identify or contact you. For example, your name, physical or electronic mail address, and telephone number would likely be considered Personally Identifiable Information. Personally Identifiable Information does not include aggregated information that, by itself, does not permit the identification of individual persons. The Activity Information would not be Personally Identifiable Information.

3. Activity Logging and Cookies

When you use the Mission Grey Site, Mission Grey records how the site is used (the "Activity Information") with its own analytics. The Mission Grey Site loads no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no such service receives the Activity Information. The site is served by a hosting provider acting for Mission Grey.

For each page you view, Mission Grey's own platform at app.missiongrey.com records the following in Mission Grey's own database: the page address, the website that linked to it (its domain name only), any campaign tags in the link you followed, your country, your device type (desktop, mobile or tablet), and how long the page was visible on your screen.

Your IP address and your browser's identification string are used at the moment of the visit to find your country and device type, to filter out automated traffic, and to form a visitor code that changes every day. They are not stored with the Activity Information. Like any web server, Mission Grey's servers also keep access logs of requests, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting. Country is looked up on Mission Grey's own servers and nothing about you is sent to the database's provider: IP Geolocation by DB-IP, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Mission Grey uses the Activity Information for internal purposes only, such as administering and improving the Mission Grey Site and understanding which pages are read and how visitors find the site.

Until you make a choice, the Mission Grey Site sets no cookie and stores nothing on your device. On your first visit the site asks whether Mission Grey may set its own cookie to recognize your browser when you come back.

CookieSet whenHoldsExpires

With mg_vid, Mission Grey can see how many visitors return and how often; the code is stored only in a scrambled, one-way form. If you decline, your visits are still counted in the anonymous daily way described above. Both cookies are Mission Grey's own: used only by the Mission Grey Site, never shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Some browsers delete them sooner, Safari for example after a week without a visit. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the foot of every page, or by deleting the cookies in your browser.

Mission Grey retains Activity Information for 13 months and IP addresses for 90 days; aggregated statistics, which do not permit the identification of individual persons, are retained indefinitely.

4. Request to Access and Delete Your Personally Identifiable Information

You have the right to request Mission Grey disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information Mission Grey has collected about you and the right to request that Mission Grey deletes any Personally Identifiable Information that Mission Grey has collected about you. Mission Grey will use reasonable efforts to remove your Personally Identifiable Information from our then current Mission Grey Site as soon as reasonably practicable. Please get in touch with us with your request via our contact page.

5. New Uses of Personal Information

Before Mission Grey uses Personally Identifiable Information for any new purpose not originally authorized by you, Mission Grey will notify you about the new purpose and allow you to opt out.

6. Changes to Mission Grey's Privacy Policy

Mission Grey may revise this Privacy Policy at its sole discretion or review it periodically. If there are any material changes to this Policy, Mission Grey will deem you accept this Policy by the continued use of Mission Grey's Site. We will post any adjustments to the Privacy Policy on this web page, and the revised version will be effective immediately when it is posted (or upon notice as applicable).

7. Miscellaneous

Mission Grey has implemented and follows appropriate technical measures to protect against the risks of unauthorized access to, erroneous disclosure of, and unlawful interception of Personally Identifiable Information. However, you should know that Mission Grey cannot entirely eliminate such risks. Mission Grey hopes this Privacy Policy clarifies Mission Grey's procedures regarding its collection, use, and disclosure of your information, including Personally Identifiable Information. From time to time, Mission Grey may modify this Privacy Policy. Such modifications will be effective when Mission Grey posts the revised statement to the Mission Grey Site. Questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted via our contact page.
