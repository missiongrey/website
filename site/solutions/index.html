How adoption works

Adoption pathIllustrative view

01 / ONE DECISION

Start with a decision.

Bring one difficult decision, opportunity, investment, market question or risk. Mission Grey builds the external context, scenarios and recommendations around it.

  • Clear situation overview
  • Impact analysis for your exposure
  • Concrete recommendations, ready for the room

02 / CONTINUOUS INTELLIGENCE

Keep it continuously current.

Questions that keep coming back become trackers, indicators, scenarios, triggers and alerts: markets, technology, regulation, suppliers, country conditions.

  • Continuous signals against your own exposure
  • Scenarios that update as conditions move
  • Thresholds, triggers and decision alerts

03 / ORGANIZATIONAL INTELLIGENCE

Make it part of the organization.

Shared dashboards, role‑specific applications, agents, APIs and workflows, built on the same intelligence layer rather than beside it.

  • Role‑specific views on shared evidence
  • Applications and dashboards around your decisions
  • Agents and API into the systems you already run

How each function uses the same layer.

Top management

Whether strategy, investment and capital allocation still hold between planning cycles.

Sales

Where is demand moving before it appears in our pipeline?

Track changing customer economics, regulation, investment, competition and technology adoption.

Operations

What external development could affect our ability to deliver?

Which suppliers, sites and routes need a decision as tariffs, energy, logistics and regulation move.

Finance / treasury

Which external change is altering our economics?
