In practice

What the work looks like

Test decisions before the world tests them.

Scenario testing

Stress‑testing that goes past the financial model.

Scenarios are built on real‑world events and run with the actors who would actually be in the room. What comes back is not a single number but a set of paths, including the ones your model would not have priced.

  • Scenario simulation based on real‑world events
  • Stress testing beyond financial models
  • Evaluation of second‑ and third‑order effects
  • War‑game style exploration of outcomes

Decision environments

Intelligence you can work in, not only read.

Mission Grey delivers intelligence as interactive environments as well as reports: explore signals and developments, test scenarios and assumptions, and evaluate decisions in real time. Custom dashboards and decision environments are created in minutes, not months.
